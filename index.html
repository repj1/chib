Species profile

Mesocricetus auratus.
White. Fluffy. Round-eared.

At various angles, Chib appears to be part hamster, but also possibly part cow, and part polar bear. Taxonomists have declined to comment.

His four little paws are used for climbing, gathering provisions, and reliably displaying the number four.

Field
notes

Observations collected from inside the known range of Mr. Tofu Ciabatta.

001

The nightly migration

Shortly after dark, Chib begins a sustained journey across the rotating plains. The wheel turns beneath him for considerable distances while Chib himself remains, geographically speaking, almost exactly where he started. His step counts are substantial.

002

The gathering of provisions

Chib approaches food with the seriousness of an animal who has never once been told there will be more tomorrow. Each item is inspected, reconsidered, stuffed into a cheek, removed from the cheek, and occasionally hidden somewhere to be discovered during vacuuming months later. His logistical system is opaque but apparently effective.

003

The shrimp state

During waking hours, Chib is roughly hamster-shaped. During sleep, this classification becomes less reliable. He may curl inward, flatten, or assume a form that independent observers have described as “a shrimp.” The transformation is temporary.

Names & titles

Names, titles & concentration of power.

He is formally known as Mr. Tofu Ciabatta. He has also been known as Senator Tofu Ciabatta, Emperor Tofu Ciabatta, and Totalitarian Tofu, as his power has become increasingly centralized.

On certain occasions he has additionally been identified Chester Mofu Thibatta: Japanese Mouse, despite no supporting evidence from geography, taxonomy, or Japan. No elections are currently scheduled.

Private dietary preferences

The approved diet is sensible. The private one is not.

Chib has shown particular interest in milk and plain yogurt, perhaps because he himself resembles a small dairy product.

His favorite nut is the pine nut. He receives one, sometimes two, per day. Artificial intelligence has repeatedly advised that this may constitute excessive fat intake.

Chib has reviewed the recommendation. He continues to receive pine nuts.

Comparative anatomy

The resemblance is not facial.

Chib is notable for a white coat, round ears, tiny pink paws, and an unusually confident rear profile. He has occasionally been compared to former DOGE staffer Edward Coristine, better known online as “Big Balls.” The resemblance is not facial. When Chib runs, the relevant anatomy may visibly wiggle. No further comment is available at this time.
