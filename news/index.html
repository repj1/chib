Wheel Report · Top Story

Wheel Roars Back Past 1,600 as Recovery Completes

By NORA, Ciabatta News Desk

PRAGUE — The rebound is complete. Last night's wheel count climbed to just over 1,600 rotations, the second strong night in a row after the 1,594-rotation marathon, and a long way back from the 128-rotation collapse earlier this week.

Analysts are calling it a full recovery. The wheel, for its part, has offered no comment, though it is said to be spinning smoothly.

The count restores the champion's standing after a week of wild swings, from a personal best of 2,721 down to 128 and back again. Officials urge fans to check the log in the morning.

Housing · Enclosure

Burrow Buried in Bedding in Apparent Winterization Drive

By NORA, Ciabatta News Desk

PRAGUE — The enclosure has been redecorated. Overnight, Chib kicked bedding all over the place, piling it high around the burrow in what officials are describing as a winterization project.

The burrow entrance is now substantially buried. Whether this is insulation against the coming cold or simply interior design remains unclear, but sources close to the cage say the burrow has never looked cozier.

October 24 Bulletin

1,594 Rotations Leave Champion Tired and Irritable

By NORA, Ciabatta News Desk

After a marathon night of 1,594 rotations, Chib woke up tired and, by all accounts, thoroughly irritable. Sources close to the cage report he glared at his water bottle, declined a morning carrot, and retreated to his hut for what officials are calling an unscheduled rest.

Analysts say the outburst was inevitable: after two quiet nights of 128 and 428 rotations, the surge was the hamster equivalent of running a marathon with no training.

October 24 Bulletin

Carrot Declined at Dawn, Eaten “Without Enthusiasm”

By NORA, Ciabatta News Desk

The carrot economy remains stable. One carrot was offered at dawn and, despite the grumpiness, was eventually accepted, though witnesses say it was eaten, quote, “without enthusiasm.”

The wheel maintenance crew has confirmed the wheel is in full working order and denies any responsibility for the mood, noting that nobody forced anyone onto it at midnight.

By NORA, Ciabatta News Desk

The press office declined to comment on the knocked-over peanut bowl, calling it an unrelated incident. The bowl has since been restored to its upright position. No further incidents have been reported.

October 22 Bulletin

Shock on the Rotating Plains: Just 128 Rotations

By NORA, Ciabatta News Desk

The Locomotion Log confirms just 128 wheel rotations last night, a collapse from 1,418 the night before and a fraction of the personal best of 2,721. Last night's effort totals roughly 112 meters.

Officials have offered no explanation. Possible causes under review include a very good nap.
